Notes & data sources
  • Software runs are all-time totals (no monthly grain in source view); the year filter does not apply to software runs — they always show the all-time figure.
  • EOS downloads are from the EOS Usage silver view. Regional breakdown is approximate: each account is assigned its primary simulation market (most runs) to derive a region.
  • Video plays are not linked to a region or market in the source — they show an all-regions total and are excluded from the regional breakdown and market detail.
  • Workshops include SF case types Workshop and Content_workshop. Email Support Cases covers all other case types (Analyst_support, General Support, etc.). Regional breakdown uses each account's primary simulation market.
  • Webinar & Group Meeting markets carry their own labels; regional figures use the market-to-region mapping.
  • Regional filter applies proportionally for downloads, workshops and email cases (all-time regional share applied to the filtered period total). Exact month-by-region data is not pre-computed.
  • Market detail is available for software runs, webinars and group meetings only. Figures are all-time totals.
  • Live data from Fabric — served with a 1-hour server cache. Use ?bust=1 to force refresh.
